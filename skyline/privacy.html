Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Skyline Computers, Lasjan, Srinagar

1. Introduction

Privacy Policy ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, submit admission inquiries, communicate with us via WhatsApp or phone, or utilize our Certificate Verification Portal.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly provided by you during your interaction with our institute portal:

  • Enquiry & Admission Forms: Full Name, Phone / WhatsApp number, preferred timing, and course interest (e.g., ADCA, DCA, Tally Prime + GST, Web Development).
  • Student Portal Verification: Registration Numbers or Certificate IDs entered into the Certificate Verification Portal to confirm diploma authenticity.
  • Direct Communication: Information provided when contacting us directly via email (skylinecomputer01@gmail.com) or helpline phone numbers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate academic and operational administrative purposes:

  • To process course inquiries and assist with batch admission scheduling.
  • To contact prospective students regarding batch start dates, counseling, and class timings.
  • To verify academic credentials and issue official diploma certifications.
  • To maintain internal student academic records and course completion tracking.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We value your trust. Your contact details and personal records remain strictly confidential. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party marketing agencies. All student records submitted through the website are handled with appropriate administrative safeguards.

5. Certificate Verification Portal

Our website features an online verification portal to help employers and students verify diploma authenticity. Entering a valid Roll Number or Certificate ID displays verified public academic information (Student Name, Father's Name, Course Awarded, Duration, and Grade). This service is provided solely for credential validation.
