Student Code of Conduct

Standards of Behavior & Rules for All Enrolled Students • Skyline Computers, Lasjan, Srinagar

1. Purpose & Standards

At Code of Conduct, we strive to maintain a disciplined, productive, and respectful academic environment. All students enrolled in diploma and certificate programs (including ADCA, DCA, Tally Prime + GST, Web Development, and Stenography) are required to follow this Code of Conduct at all times while present on campus premises.

2. Computer Lab & Hardware Usage Rules

To maintain our high-standard hardware facility and respect the "One Student - One PC" policy:

  • Assigned Workstations: Students must sit at their designated computer system and are not permitted to change wiring, disconnect peripherals, or tamper with system hardware.
  • Software Integrity: Installing unauthorized third-party software, games, or modifying system settings/passwords is strictly prohibited.
  • Food & Beverages: Eating, drinking, or carrying liquids into the computer lab area is completely forbidden to prevent damage to equipment.

3. Attendance, Punctuality & Batch Discipline

  • Timeliness: Students must arrive on time for their designated batch shift (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening). Late entries disrupt class lectures.
  • Minimum Attendance Requirement: A minimum of 75% attendance is required to sit for final module examinations and receive official course certificates.
  • Leave Applications: Prior written notice or WhatsApp communication is required for consecutive absences exceeding 3 days.

4. Professional Behavior & Campus Decorum

Students must demonstrate respect toward faculty members, administrative staff, and fellow students:

  • Mutual Respect: Any form of harassment, verbal abuse, bullying, or discrimination will result in immediate suspension.
  • Mobile Device Usage: Mobile phones must be switched to silent mode during theory lectures and practical lab sessions. Personal phone calls must be taken outside class space.
  • Identity & Verification: Students must carry their institute enrollment card/fee slip while on institute grounds.

5. Disciplinary Action & Violations

Failure to adhere to the code of conduct may result in progressive disciplinary measures:

  1. First Warning: Verbal reminder issued by the faculty or batch counselor.
  2. Second Warning: Written notice issued and notification sent to parents/guardians.
  3. Termination of Enrollment: Severe misconduct, vandalism, or non-compliance may lead to immediate cancellation of admission without fee refund.
